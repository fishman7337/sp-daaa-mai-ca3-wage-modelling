# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is maintained for coursework, learning, and reproducible analysis. Everyone participating should communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, or deliberate disruption.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be clear, kind, and specific when giving feedback.
- Credit sources, tools, and contributors honestly.
- Keep academic integrity at the centre of any reuse or extension.
- Raise problems early when data, code, or report claims appear inconsistent.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or targeted personal attacks.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of authorship, or removal of attribution.
- Sharing private credentials, personal data, or restricted course materials beyond their intended use.
- Deliberately submitting changes that break tests, conceal errors, or falsify results.

## Enforcement

For coursework use, concerns should be raised with the project owner or the relevant Singapore Polytechnic teaching staff. The expected response is to review the concern, preserve evidence where needed, and correct or remove harmful material.
